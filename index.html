Find what carries the load.

FIELD GUIDE

How to grow the sky

Walk with W A S D (arrow keys work too). Space jumps; hold Shift to run. Drag to orbit your view and scroll to zoom. On touch devices, use the movement pad and drag the sky to look.

Choose seeds using 1–4 or the bottom bar. Stand on the island where you want to act and press E or Sow Seed. Green seeds grow a wide walkable vine to the next island. When it flowers, walk across—or press F to follow it safely.

Seeds are earned as you reach new islands. Gold roots stabilize a shared pin, blue pulses awaken the hollow heart, and pink blooms decorate the path. If you fall, the garden catches you at your latest island.

The world reshapes with its URL seed. It keeps the story solvable while the islands, flowers and details change.

The world needs WebGL2.

Try Chrome, Edge, or Firefox with hardware acceleration enabled.
